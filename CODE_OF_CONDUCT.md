# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling,
publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to [support@isogate.tech](mailto:support@isogate.tech).
Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical. We will
take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that
violates this code.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
